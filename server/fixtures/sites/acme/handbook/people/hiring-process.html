How we hire engineers

Our interview process has four stages and usually takes three weeks.

1. Recruiter screen (30 min)

A conversation about your background, motivation and what you want next.

2. Take-home exercise

A small payouts API exercise, about 3 hours. We review your code with you in the next stage.

3. Technical interview (90 min)

Walk us through your take-home, then a system design interview: design a ledger that never loses money, with idempotent payouts.

4. Values interview

A behavioural interview with two engineers about ownership, written communication, and how you mentor others.

We aim to make an offer decision within 3 working days of the final round.
